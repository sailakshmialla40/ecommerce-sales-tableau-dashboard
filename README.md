# E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analytics solution developed using Tableau.

The project analyzes sales, profit, orders, products, customers, customer segments, categories, sub-categories, and regional performance to provide business-focused insights through interactive dashboards.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Track key business KPIs
- Identify top-performing products and customers
- Analyze sales and profit across categories and sub-categories
- Compare regional and state-level performance
- Understand customer segment performance
- Analyze the relationship between discount and profit
- Enable interactive analysis using dashboard filters

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Tableau
- CSV
- Data Visualization
- Calculated Fields
- Aggregations
- Filters
- Top-N Analysis
- Geographic Maps

## 📈 Dashboards

### 1. E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analysis

This dashboard provides an executive-level overview of business performance.

**Key metrics:**
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Profit Margin %

**Visualizations:**
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Profit by Sub-Category

---

### 2. Product & Sales Performance Analysis

This dashboard focuses on product and geographical performance.

**Visualizations:**
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Discount vs Profit
- Sales & Profit by State
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region

---

### 3. Customer & Order Analysis

This dashboard focuses on customer segments and order performance.

**Visualizations:**
- Orders by Segment
- Sales by Segment
- Profit by Segment
- Top 10 Customers by Sales
- Sales by Sub-Category

## 🔎 Interactive Features

The dashboards include interactive filters for:

- Category
- Region
- Segment

These filters allow users to dynamically explore the data across multiple visualizations.
